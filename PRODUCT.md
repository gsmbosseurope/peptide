# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primarily biohacker and self-experimenter consumers: individuals researching peptides for personal use rather than accredited labs ordering in bulk. Less technical than a lab-procurement buyer, arriving with real skepticism about legitimacy in a category where fraud is common, and needing more reassurance and plain-language education than a purely scientific audience would. This is why the site carries a peptide guide and blog alongside the catalog rather than a bare product list.

## Product Purpose

trusted-peptide.com sells EU-sourced research peptides with HPLC-verified purity, transparent variant pricing, and wholesale discount tiers. It exists to let a self-experimenter find, trust, and order a specific compound with confidence, in a market where verifying a supplier's legitimacy is the primary purchase obstacle. Success is a completed order (or a WhatsApp conversation that leads to one) from a buyer who came in skeptical and left convinced.

## Positioning

EU-sourced research peptides, independently verified for purity, sold with transparent pricing. Every screen should reinforce that the purity and sourcing claims are real and checkable, not marketing copy.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Buy now (add to cart / order) is primary; WhatsApp contact is the secondary fallback for visitors with questions before committing.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: EU-sourced research peptides, independently verified for purity, sold with transparent pricing.
- Belief ladder: (1) this supplier is legitimate, not a scam, (2) the purity and sourcing claims are independently verifiable (HPLC, EU sourcing), (3) the specific peptide and variant needed is available at a fair price, (4) ordering is low-friction (WhatsApp or a simple checkout).
- Proof on hand: none captured yet. Testimonials, lab certificates, or partner/shipping-carrier logos should be added here (or under `.impeccable/assets/proof/`) as they become available; the current trust signals are the HPLC/purity claims and EU-seal badge already in the footer, not third-party proof.

## Brand Personality

Premium, refined, confident. High-end without being cold: restrained rather than shouting, but never hedging on the legitimacy claims that carry the sale. This sits above "clinical and cold" and above "casual and playful" — it should read like a serious, quality-first supplier a skeptical buyer can trust, not a lifestyle brand and not a sterile lab-supply catalog.

## Anti-references

No specific reference sites were named. Default guidance: avoid anything that reads as a low-trust reseller (stock-photo hero, generic "Acme"-style branding, cluttered upsells) — the category already has enough of that, and it's precisely what the legitimacy belief has to overcome.

## Design Principles

- Legitimacy before persuasion: every page should make the purity/sourcing claims feel checkable, not asserted. Prefer specifics (HPLC, EU sourcing, cert marks) over adjectives.
- Plain language over jargon: the audience is informed but not clinical; explain rather than assume prior expertise (this is what the peptide guide and blog exist to do).
- Confidence without volume: premium and refined means restraint, not maximal polish or loud visual flourish.
- Low-friction path to WhatsApp: for a buyer who isn't ready to check out, the secondary CTA needs to stay easy to find, not buried behind the primary.
- One brand across languages: the English and Arabic (RTL) surfaces are the same brand, not two different products; parity in tone and trust signals matters as much as parity in layout.
